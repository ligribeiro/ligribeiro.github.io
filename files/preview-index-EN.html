About me

I'm Ligia Ribeiro. 15 years in design, the last 6 focused on digital products. I went from communication (Advertising at PUC-SP) to software development (FATEC), and that intersection is where I feel at home: understanding people, designing the solution and writing the code that ships it.

I like working side by side with product managers, research and engineering, from design system tokens to deploying an automation. I believe in products that solve real problems, with method and craft.

When I close the code editor, I illustrate in watercolor and digital media under the Margarida Negra brand, with a soft spot for projects that bring science closer to people.

the site as a case study

This site is a design system with two skins. Switch and see.

switch skin

One component set, two skins over the same dark ink: orange primary for product and design work, violet primary for development and automation. The tokens on the side are read live from computed CSS: switch the skin and watch the values change. It's the discipline I applied to the Compsis design system, now applied to myself.
